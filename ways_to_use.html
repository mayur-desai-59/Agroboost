Ways to Use Fertilizers

  • Choose the right fertilizer: Select the appropriate fertilizer based on the type of plants you're growing.
  • Follow the instructions: Always follow the recommended dosage for your plants.
  • Apply evenly: Ensure an even distribution of fertilizer around the plant base.
  • Water after application: Water the plants after applying fertilizers to help them absorb the nutrients.

Safety Measures

  • Wear protective gear: Always wear gloves, goggles, and a mask to avoid direct contact with fertilizers.
  • Apply fertilizers in the right amount: Overuse of fertilizers can damage plants and the environment.
  • Avoid applying fertilizers on windy days: Wind can blow fertilizers onto unintended areas.
  • Store fertilizers safely: Keep fertilizers in a cool, dry place, out of reach of children and pets.
  • Wash hands after use: Always wash hands thoroughly after handling fertilizers.
